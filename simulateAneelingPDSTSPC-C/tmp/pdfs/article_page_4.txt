2. The Parallel Drone T raveling Salesman Problem with Collective
Drones
In this section, we formally describe the PDSTSP-c, as originally introduced
in [17], and we present a new Constraint Programming model. We start from the
single-vehicle problem because its description helps in introducing the multiple-
vehicle version.
2.1. Problem Description

with vertex 0 representing the depot, and the remaining vertices being associated
with the customers (set C = V \ {0}). Each customer i requests delivery of a
parcel of weight wi from the depot. The fleet of vehicles available for deliveries
is a driver-operated delivery truck, with unlimited range and capacity, and a
set D of m homogeneous drones that are based at the depot and equipped with
