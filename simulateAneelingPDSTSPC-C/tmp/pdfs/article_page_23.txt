5. Conclusions
In this paper, we have discussed several advances for the Parallel Drone
Scheduling Traveling Salesman Problem with cooperative drones. In particu-
lar, we have proposed a Constraint Programming model coupled with a valid
inequality that allows us to find improved lower and upper bounds for the in-
stances proposed in the literature. Additionally, we demonstrated that the pro-
posed valid inequality can be used to enhance the performance of other methods
such as MILP models.
We have also extended the problem into the new Parallel Drone Schedul-
ing Vehicle Routing Problem with cooperative drones, where several trucks are
available. For this new extension, we have proposed two alternative Constraint
Programming models and a Mixed Integer Programming model. Experimental
results suggest that Constraint Programming guarantees better performance,
but seems to have scaling issues on large instances, leaving room for future
studies on heuristic approaches tailored to the problem.
