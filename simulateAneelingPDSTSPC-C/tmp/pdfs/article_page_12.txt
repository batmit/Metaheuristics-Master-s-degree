The model minimizes the time to serve all the customers (51). Constraints
(52) force α to be larger than any tour of the trucks, and inequalities (53)
guarantee that α is larger than the completion time of any drone’s mission time.
Equations (54) assign customers from CF to either a drone or a truck, while
constraints (55) force the customers that can be visited only by a truck ( CT )
to receive such a visit. Inequalities (56) impose that a customer can be visited
by a truck only if it is in use. Equalities (57) are flow conservation constraints
for the truck tours. Inequalities (58) are subtour elimination constraints [1] and
guarantee that truck tours are circuits including the depot. Note that these
constraints are exponential in number, depending on any possible subset H of
C. In our implementation, they will be generated dynamically as described in
Section 3.4.1 below. Constraints (59) refers to the flow of drones and guarantee
that the number of drones going from customer i to j must be lower than m,
only if there is a flow from i to j. Inequalities (60) regulate completion of
