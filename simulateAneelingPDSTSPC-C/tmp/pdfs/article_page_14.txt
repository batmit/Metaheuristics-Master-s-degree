4.2. PDSTSP-c
In this section, we aim at comparing the results obtained by solving the
CP 1 model described in Section 2.2, with and without the valid inequality (16).
The results are summarized in Table 1 for the small instances and in Table
2 for the large ones. We compare CP 1 with the methods introduced in [17],
namely a Mixed Integer Linear Programming (MILP) model solved with IBM
CPLEX 12.1 [8] and two versions of a Ruin&Recreate metaheuristic: RnR fast
and RnR. Note that the results of the MILP model in [17] are only available
for small instances and those reported for the Ruin&Recreate methods are the
best over 30 runs. To fully understand the impact of inequality (16), we also
considered the MILP model described in Section 3.4 for the PDSVRP-c and run
it with s = 1 (one truck only) as well as the inequality (16). This method is run
on small instances only, since a previous study ([17]) showed MILP models are
not suitable for the large instances.
