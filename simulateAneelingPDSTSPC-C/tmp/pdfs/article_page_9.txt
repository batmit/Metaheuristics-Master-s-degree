The constraints strictly follow the meaning already described for the CP 1
model in Section 2.2. The only changes are as follows. Constraint (18) now
defines α based on the time required by each truck to go back to the depot after
visiting each of its assigned customers. This constraint is valid since the travel
times satisfy the triangular property, although it could be made valid also for the
general case with the use of a OnlyEnforceIf statement (see below). Constraint
(21) describes a set of circuits through the MultipleCircuit command of CP-SAT
([22]) to reflect we are now dealing with several tours instead of one. The new
constraint (22) forces the number of tours to be maximum s. Whereas the new
constraints (23) calculate the service start time for each customer visited by a
truck (remember that = ⇒ indicates the OnlyEnforceIf, which activates the
constraint iif xij = 1).
